Open source iOS 26 app for Spotify

The line being sung, wherever you glance.

Spotify shows lyrics inside its own app and nowhere else. Lyricar follows your Spotify playback and keeps the current line on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, in widgets and on CarPlay.

CarPlay

Karaoke, Focus, Stage and Track tabs. CarPlay always highlights the first row, so the list window moves with the song and the highlighted row is the line being sung. Tap a row to jump there. Real cars need Apple's CarPlay entitlement; the Simulator works without it.

Staying in sync

Ask Spotify, then trust a local clock.

Spotify only says where a song is when you ask. Lyricar asks once a second while you look at the phone or CarPlay and every 3 seconds behind the lock screen. In between, a 10 Hz local clock moves the highlight. Each answer is weighted by its round trip, so one slow reply cannot drag the clock off.

  2. PlaybackClocksamples pinned to the request midpoint, the most precise one wins

Build it yourself

Clone, configure, run.

You need a Mac with Xcode 26, Homebrew, an iPhone on iOS 26 or just the Simulator, and a Spotify account. Playback controls need Spotify Premium.

  1. 1 Clone

    Get the source.

    git clone https://github.com/noluyorAbi/lyricar && cd lyricar

  2. 2 Configure

    XcodeGen writes the project. The setup script asks for your team, a bundle prefix and your Spotify client ID.

    brew install xcodegen && ./scripts/setup.sh

  3. 3 Run

    Pick the Lyricar scheme, then your iPhone or a Simulator.

    open Lyricar.xcodeproj

Spotify Development Mode. New Spotify apps allow at most 5 users, each added by hand, and the owner needs Premium. That is enough for you and a few friends, and it is why Lyricar is not an App Store app.

Free Apple ID. A Personal Team build runs for 7 days; run scripts/install-device.sh again to renew it.

Lyrics. They come from LRCLIB, a community database. A missing or badly timed song can be fixed there; the per-track nudge covers small offsets meanwhile.

What leaves the phone

Two services, nothing else.

  • Spotify Web API gets your token from the Keychain and answers what is playing. Sign-in is Authorization Code with PKCE, so there is no client secret.
  • LRCLIB gets the track name, artist, album and duration, to find lyrics.
  • No backend, no analytics, no account. The diagnostics log stays in the app until you pull it.
